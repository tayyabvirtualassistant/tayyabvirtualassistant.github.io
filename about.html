My Story

I'm Tayyab, a virtual assistant based in Lahore, Pakistan, working with entrepreneurs, coaches, and small business owners across the globe. What began as freelance administrative work grew into a full practice built around one idea: businesses run better when the right tasks are handled by the right person.

I got into virtual assistance after watching founders spend hours each week on work that didn't need their expertise — inbox sorting, scheduling, research — while the strategic work that actually grows a business sat untouched. If your time is worth $100 an hour, spending it on $10-an-hour tasks doesn't make sense, and that's the gap I fill.

My work is guided by a few non-negotiables: attention to detail, professional communication, strict confidentiality, and reliability. Clients should never have to wonder whether something got done — they should just see that it did.

I'm not looking for one-off tasks. I'm looking for long-term partnerships with people who want a dependable extension of their team, not just a name on an invoice.
