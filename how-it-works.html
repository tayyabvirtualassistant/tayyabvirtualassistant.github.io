How It Works

From first message to results in days, not weeks.

1
Day 1
You Reach Out

You send a message via the contact form, WhatsApp, or email. Briefly describe what you need help with — no formal brief required at this stage.

Day 1–2
We Have a Free 15-Minute Call

We schedule a short video call using Zoom or Google Meet. I listen to your business needs, current bottlenecks, and the tasks you want handled. No sales pitch — just an honest conversation.

2
3
Day 2–3
You Receive a Clear Proposal

Within 24 hours, I send a detailed written proposal covering services, scope, deliverables, pricing, and expected outcomes. Everything written clearly — no vague language, no hidden fees.

Day 3–5
Quick and Smooth Onboarding

Once agreed, we set up shared tools, access credentials, communication channels, and document your workflow preferences. Most clients are fully operational in under 48 hours.

4
5
Day 5 onward
Tasks Handled. You Focus on Growth.

I begin handling your tasks with daily progress updates. You receive a weekly summary report every Friday covering work completed, hours used, and upcoming tasks.
